# SimplyMirror Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

SimplyMirror shows your Android phone screen on your Windows PC over a USB cable.

## Data collection

SimplyMirror **collects nothing**. No account, no analytics, no ads, no location,
no contacts, no tracking of any kind. The app works fully offline after setup.

## Network use

- The SkyDrop edition downloads the free PC helper (`SimplyMirror.exe`) once from
  our public GitHub release page. No personal data is sent.
- The optional on-phone setup page can be served over your own WiFi between your
  own phone and PC. Nothing leaves your network.

## Permissions used (and why)

- **Internet / network state** — one-time download of the PC helper.
- **USB connection state** — to detect the cable and guide setup.
- **Storage (older Android only)** — to place the PC helper in Downloads so your
  computer can see it over USB.
