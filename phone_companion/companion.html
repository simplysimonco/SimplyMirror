📱 Mirror Helper
Phone setup — do once, 5 minutes

1Turn on the hidden switch

Your phone hides a switch called USB debugging. It just means “let my computer show my screen”. It is safe on your own computer.

Open Settings for me

If that button does nothing, do it by hand:
Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times → go back → System → Developer options → turn USB debugging ON.

2Plug the cable correctly

Use the cable that came with your phone. Some cheap cables only charge — they will not work.

Plug phone → computer. On your phone, swipe down from the top, tap USB / Charging → choose File Transfer.

3Say “Allow” on your phone

Your phone will pop up: “Allow USB debugging?”

Tick Always allow → tap Allow.

No popup? Unplug, plug again, then look at your phone. Unlock it first.

4Back to computer

On your computer, click the big green button SHOW MY PHONE ON PC NOW. Your screen + sound appear.

🎉 All done! From now on just plug in + click one button.
Keep this helper like an app:

Chrome: tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Add.
Samsung Internet: tap ☰ → Add page to → Home screen.
